# Maintainers

OpenQA is currently maintained by the `openqa-cn` organization. Maintainer names and areas of responsibility will be listed here as the project grows.

Maintainers are responsible for technical review, release quality, security response, and keeping public claims aligned with shipped capabilities.

## Decision making

Issues and pull requests are the primary public record. For changes to the evidence schema, contribution policy, license, or commercial boundary, open a design discussion before implementation.
